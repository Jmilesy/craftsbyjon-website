Custom & Bespoke Orders

Want something made just for you — your own logo, or a design built around a hobby or interest? Tell us what you have in mind below and we'll come back with a quote.

What we can do

✓ Yes, we can

  • Adapt your own logo or artwork (send us a JPEG/PNG/PDF — even a slightly rough one is usually fine) onto bookmarks, keyrings, or both
  • Create an original design around a theme, hobby or interest (e.g. "my dad's a keen fisherman")
  • Personalise each item in the batch differently — different names, initials or small details on a shared design

✗ Sorry, not this

  • Personalised illustrations or portraits of a specific person (e.g. "a cartoon of my dad fishing on a riverbank") — this applies at any order size, it's just not something we offer

Pricing

Items 1–10£5.00 each
Each item after the first 10£3.50 each

This is a running total, not a threshold — the first 10 items are always £5.00 each, and it's only anything beyond that which drops to £3.50. For example: 10 items = £50.00. 11 items = £53.50 (10 × £5.00 + 1 × £3.50). Minimum order: 5 items per bespoke design. Orders below 5, or requests needing unusually heavy design work, are quoted individually — just tell us what you need in the form and we'll let you know.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below with what you'd like and send over any logo/artwork by email if you have it.
  2. We'll come back to you with a price and rough timeline — usually within a couple of days.
  3. Once you're happy, we'll send a payment link to secure your order.
  4. We'll make up a design proof for you to approve before anything's printed.
  5. Your order is made, packaged and posted.
