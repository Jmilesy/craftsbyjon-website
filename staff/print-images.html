Print Images
Attaches the Gelato mockup pictures (plain shot, living room, bedroom) to the print products, one set per Frame, Size and Text combination, so the store can show the right picture as a customer changes size or frame. You can drop several Gelato mockup zips at once. Nothing on this page sends anything to Gelato or changes a price or a listing.
1. Drop the mockup zips
Drop the zips anywhere on this page (or click the box). The page reads them on your computer first and shows what it found before anything uploads. Big zips take a little while to read.
🗂️
Click to choose or drag the Gelato mockup .zip files here
2. Check what was found
One row per print. "Found" is how many Frame, Size and Text combinations the dropped zips cover out of that product's full set. Pictures are shrunk to 1600 px on your computer before they upload.
