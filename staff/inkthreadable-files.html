Inkthreadable Files
The print-ready file for each product Inkthreadable makes, and the switch that hands that product over to automatic ordering. Files are stored privately on the server, never on the public image folder. Uploading a file here does not place an order. Once a product is switched on, each paid order creates an unpaid order at Inkthreadable for you to check and pay on their site. Nothing is made or charged until you pay it.
Switching a product on also changes what customers see for it: checkout shows the free “Printed to Order” delivery, there is no gift option on a cart of only made-to-order items, and its stock count stops going down (it is made to order). Customer status emails start separately, when the go-live date is set on the server.
