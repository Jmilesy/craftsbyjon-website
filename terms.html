4. Ordering

Orders are placed through our website checkout powered by Stripe. You will receive an email confirmation after payment. We aim to dispatch within 1–2 business days.

5. Shipping

UK only. Handmade items are shipped via Royal Mail:

  • 2nd Class — 2–5 business days
  • 1st Class — 1–2 business days

Print-on-demand items (such as mugs) are fulfilled and shipped by our print partner via Evri, typically within 7–10 days.

Shipping costs are shown at checkout.

You have the right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving your goods under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. This right does not apply to personalised or custom-made items.

To cancel, email jon@craftsbyjon.co.uk. Return postage is the customer's responsibility unless the item is faulty. Refunds are processed within 14 days of receiving the returned item.

7. Faulty Items

If your item arrives damaged or faulty, please email jon@craftsbyjon.co.uk with a photo within 7 days of receipt. We will offer a replacement or full refund including postage.
