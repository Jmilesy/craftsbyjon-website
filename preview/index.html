What's real and what's pretend

  • Real: products, photos, prices, options, descriptions, delivery rates and dispatch times, returns wording, the four testimonials, your About text.
  • Pretend: the basket (stored only in this browser, checkout switched off), "Jon's picks" (my choice, you'd pick these), "New in" (totes and coaster set B, from the handbook dates, because there's no "listed on" date yet), and "For book lovers" (all bookmarks, as a stand-in for products you'd tick).
  • Hidden because empty: Birthday gifts and Christmas & seasonal. They appear automatically once you assign products to them.
  • Not shown yet: photo upload (the birthday photo mug isn't marked Ready to Go), mug text colour, print pictures changing with frame choice. The live pickers move across unchanged in the build.

Facebook arrivals

Last 60 days of tracked Facebook links: about 72% went to a single product (mostly Retro Deal mugs and coasters) and about 28% to the Retro Deal collection. Almost none went to the homepage. Today those product links land on a summary page that needs another tap to reach the shop. So:

  • Product page first. Buy straight from the page you land on: photo, price, options and the Add button all within the first screen on a phone, with no popup in the way.
  • A "More from Retro Deal" link right under the delivery line, so someone who came for a mug finds the coasters and prints.
  • The sticky Add to basket bar appears once you scroll past the main button.
  • The same page for everyone. No special Facebook-only version, so tracking stays simple and nothing breaks if a link is shared on.

Shop-wide lineAcrylic bookmarks and keyrings I make by hand, plus mugs, tees, prints and totes with my designs, printed to order.

Why this wording: the transfers are printed by your UV DTF supplier and you apply, cut and finish them, so "I apply the design... cut, finish and pack" is accurate where "I print" wouldn't be. *Nine Summer keyrings are marked as not original designs (licensed artwork), so "Original design by me" only shows when original_design is Yes. Suppliers are never named (white label). Checked against the handbook's banned-words list. This replaces "Made by hand, by one person (me!)... not a factory" in the current store header, which currently sits above the printed products too.

Meta tracking

There isn't any. No Meta pixel, no Conversions API and no Facebook catalogue feed anywhere: not in the website, not in the VPS services, not in n8n (one inactive "Product Catalog Demo" workflow), not in git history. Facebook traffic is measured by your own /src/fb-... links and the first-party funnel log, which already works without cookies. My recommendation is to leave it that way for now: a pixel mostly helps paid ads, which the business rules out, and it would need another line in the cookie banner and privacy policy.

How this preview is kept away from the live shop

  • It lives in a separate folder on the server, a copy of the working branch, not the live site's folder. The live shop's files are untouched.
  • Behind the staff password, marked noindex so search engines ignore it.
  • It reads a saved copy of the catalogue. It never calls the products API, never sends funnel events, never loads Google Analytics, Clarity or Pinterest, and can't start a Stripe checkout.
  • The pretend basket uses its own storage key, so it can't touch a real basket in the same browser.
