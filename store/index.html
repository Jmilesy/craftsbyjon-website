I make things. In Deal. In chaos.

I'm Jon, based in Deal, Kent, and this is my outlet for what I like doing best: designing and crafting. Whether that's a bookmark or keyring, mug or sticker, or anything in between.

It started in spring 2026, flat on my back after ankle surgery, needing something to do that wasn't just staring at the ceiling. The Bee Collection came first. Then I couldn't stop.

Everything starts at £3.50. Handmade bookmarks and keyrings go by Royal Mail from £1.55, and 2nd Class is free when your order comes to £10 or more. Printed items are made to order and sent straight to you, with postage included in the price. Designed and run by me in Deal, Kent. Some gifts are handmade here, while others are printed to order by trusted production partners. Just me, a bad ankle that's mostly better now, and a slightly manic grin.
