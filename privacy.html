Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Registered business: Crafts by Jon, Deal, Kent, United Kingdom

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how I (Crafts by Jon) collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you interact with me — including through my website (craftsbyjon.co.uk), my Facebook Page, my Instagram account, and any automated services I operate using the Meta Platform APIs.

I am committed to handling all data responsibly and in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

2. Who I Am

Crafts by Jon is a small handmade goods business based in Deal, Kent, UK, selling acrylic bookmarks and keyrings, plus print-on-demand items such as mugs, coasters, t-shirts and prints. The data controller for the purposes of UK GDPR is Jon (the sole owner). Contact: jon@craftsbyjon.co.uk.

3. What Data I Collect and Why

3.1 Website Visitors

I use cookies for website analytics, with your consent. When you first visit the store, a banner lets you Accept or Decline analytics cookies. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set — only basic server access logs (see below) are collected. If you accept, I use:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited) — collects anonymised traffic and behaviour data (pages viewed, approximate location, device type) to help me understand how the store is used. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation) — records session behaviour (clicks, scrolling, mouse movement) and generates heatmaps to help me improve the site. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Neither tool is used for advertising, and I do not sell or share this data with third parties for marketing purposes. You can change your cookie choice at any time using the "Manage cookie preferences" link in the site footer.

I also collect basic server access logs (IP address, page requested, timestamp) for security and performance purposes. These logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days and are not used to identify individuals.

3.2 Direct Contact

If you email me at jon@craftsbyjon.co.uk, I collect your email address and the content of your message in order to respond to your enquiry. This data is retained for up to 12 months and is not shared with third parties.

3.3 Facebook Messenger (pages_messaging)

I use the Meta pages_messaging permission to receive and respond to direct messages sent to my Facebook Page. When you message my Page:

  • Your message content and Facebook-provided sender ID are received by my automated system.
  • The message is processed in real time by an AI assistant to generate a relevant reply about my products.
  • I receive a notification via my internal systems confirming the message was handled.
  • Message content is not stored beyond the immediate processing required to generate a reply.
  • I do not use message content for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than responding to your enquiry.

You can stop receiving automated replies at any time by sending "STOP" or by adjusting your Facebook Messenger settings. I comply fully with Meta's Messenger Platform policies, including responding only within the standard messaging window.

3.4 Instagram Direct Messages (instagram_manage_messages)

I use the instagram_manage_messages permission to receive and respond to direct messages sent to my Instagram Business account. The same principles as Section 3.3 apply:

  • Message content and the Instagram-provided sender ID are received for real-time processing only.
  • Replies are generated automatically to answer product enquiries.
  • Message content is not stored beyond immediate use.
  • Data is never used for targeting, advertising, or profiling.

3.5 Facebook Page Insights (read_insights)

I use the read_insights permission to access aggregated analytics for my Facebook Page, such as reach, impressions, post engagement, and follower counts. This data:

  • Is aggregated and anonymised — it does not identify individual users.
  • Is used solely to understand how my content is performing and to inform my posting strategy.
  • Is not shared with third parties or used for advertising.

3.6 Instagram Insights (instagram_manage_insights)

I use the instagram_manage_insights permission to access aggregated analytics for my Instagram Business account (reach, impressions, follower activity, etc.). The same principles as Section 3.5 apply — data is aggregated, anonymised, and used only for internal performance review.

3.7 Instagram Content Publishing (instagram_content_publish)

I use the instagram_content_publish permission to publish posts (images and captions) to my own Instagram Business account automatically, as part of my content scheduling. This permission is used to post content about my own products only. No user data is collected or used as part of this process.

3.8 Product Catalogue Management (catalog_management)

I use the catalog_management permission to manage my own product catalogue on Facebook/Instagram. This involves uploading and updating product information (names, prices, images, descriptions) that I own. No personal data belonging to users or customers is collected or processed as part of catalogue management.

3.9 Purchases & Orders

When you place an order through my store, I collect your name, email address, delivery address, and order details (items purchased, and personalisation text or delivery notes where applicable) in order to fulfil and dispatch your order. Payment itself is processed directly by Stripe — I do not see or store your full card details; Stripe provides me with a payment reference to confirm your transaction (see Section 8, Third-Party Services).

Handmade items are packed and posted by me, using Royal Mail. Print-on-demand items (such as mugs, coasters, t-shirts and prints) are made and posted by my print partners, so for those orders I pass your name, delivery address and order details to them, and they pass your name and delivery address to the carrier that delivers your parcel. My print partners are Gelato (Gelato ASA, Oslo, Norway) and Inkthreadable (Hyper Merch Limited, Blackburn, UK), and the carriers are Evri and Royal Mail. I only share the details needed to make and deliver your order (see Section 8, Third-Party Services).

Order and transaction data is retained for a minimum of 6 years to meet UK tax and accounting record-keeping requirements. Data not required for those financial obligations — such as delivery notes or personalisation preferences no longer needed once an order is complete — is removed once it is no longer necessary for any other legitimate purpose.

4. Meta Platform Data

I access certain data via the Meta Platform APIs (Facebook Graph API) strictly as required to operate the features described above. In accordance with Meta's Platform Terms:

  • I do not sell, transfer, or share Meta Platform Data with third parties.
  • I do not use Meta Platform Data to make decisions about eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, housing, or other sensitive matters.
  • I do not use Meta Platform Data to build profiles on individuals for any purpose beyond responding to their enquiries.
  • I do not combine Meta Platform Data with data from other sources for profiling or targeting.
  • API access credentials (tokens, keys) are stored securely on my private server and are never exposed publicly.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

I retain different categories of data for different periods:

  • Messenger / Instagram DM content: Not retained beyond the immediate processing window (typically seconds).
  • Insights / analytics data: Retained for up to 12 months for performance review purposes, then deleted.
  • Email correspondence: Retained for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Server access logs: Retained for up to 30 days, then automatically purged.
  • Order / transaction data: Retained for a minimum of 6 years to meet UK tax and accounting requirements. Non-financial order data (e.g. delivery notes, personalisation preferences) is removed once no longer needed for any other purpose.

If you would like me to delete any personal data I hold about you, please contact me at jon@craftsbyjon.co.uk and I will action your request within 30 days.

6. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data I hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to processing of your data.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at jon@craftsbyjon.co.uk. If you believe I have not handled your data correctly, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

7. Data Security

I implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted server storage, restricted access to API credentials, and HTTPS for all web traffic. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but I take my obligations seriously and review my security practices regularly.

8. Third-Party Services

My services make use of the following third parties. Each handles personal data under its own privacy policy:

  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) — Meta Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — payment processing and checkout. My store is self-hosted at craftsbyjon.co.uk; Stripe handles card payments and checkout security. See the Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Gelato (Gelato ASA, Norway) — print-on-demand printing and dispatch. Receives your name, delivery address and order details for print-on-demand orders. Gelato uses production partners close to the delivery address, which may be outside the UK and EU. See the Gelato Privacy Policy.
  • Inkthreadable (Hyper Merch Limited, UK) — print-on-demand printing and dispatch. Receives your name, delivery address and order details for print-on-demand orders. See the Inkthreadable Privacy Policy.
  • Royal Mail and Evri — delivery. They receive your name and delivery address to deliver your parcel, from me for handmade items (Royal Mail) or from my print partners for print-on-demand items.
  • Google Analytics 4 — website analytics, with your consent. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity — session recording and heatmaps, with your consent. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

9. Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of my services after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For significant changes, I will post a notice on my website.
